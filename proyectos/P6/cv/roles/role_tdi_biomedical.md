## DAILY WORKFLOW

1. **Start of shift:** report to assigned machine; check work order / traveler sheet (and verbal direction from supervisor).
2. **Run:** machine executes pre-loaded G-code program automatically.
3. **Inspect:** at the defined sampling interval, pull a part, verify dimensions with metrology tools.
4. **Document:** record measurements on the quality control sheet — strict, error-free formatting required.
5. **Sign and hand off:** supervisor reviews; if any error/typo, redo the entire sheet.
6. **Material/tooling:** reload raw stock, perform light deburring with metallic brushes for surface imperfections.
7. **Anomalies:** **zero operator troubleshooting allowed.** Any machine issue (jam, tool break, dimensional drift) reported immediately to the supervisor — strict regulatory protocol.

**Team coordination:** Each operator owned a single machine, but the team maintained constant situational awareness across the floor — operators covered each other during breaks, documentation, or bathroom time. Communication was central, and operators learned to use **all senses** (sight, sound, even smell of overheating tool/coolant) to detect process issues early.
