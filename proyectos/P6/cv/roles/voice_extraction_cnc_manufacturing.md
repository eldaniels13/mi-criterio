# Voice Extraction — CNC / Manufacturing Roles
**Use:** Read questions aloud in voice mode. Answer freely. AI recaps after each block.
**Companies in scope:** EncoreTools · MoldTech · TDI Tecnología & Diseño Industrial
**Output target:** `role_cnc_manufacturing.md` v1.0

---

## HOW TO USE THIS IN VOICE MODE

1. Tell Claude: *"Run CNC manufacturing role extraction using voice template"*
2. Claude reads one block at a time — you answer out loud
3. After each block, Claude recaps what it heard — you confirm or correct
4. At the end, Claude generates the role file

---

## BLOCK 1 — TIMELINE & COMPANIES

> *Get the full picture first. Don't worry about order — we'll sort it.*

1. What years were you working in CNC/manufacturing? Give me start and end for each company if you remember.
2. Were EncoreTools, MoldTech, and TDI separate jobs — or was there overlap?
3. Which one came first? Which one was most recent?
4. Were any of them part-time, freelance, or project-based?
5. What city or area were these jobs in?

---

## BLOCK 2 — COMPANY CONTEXT

> *One company at a time. Start with whichever feels clearest.*

**For each company, answer:**

6. What did this company make or do? (product, industry, clients)
7. How big was the team — were you one of few, or part of a large floor?
8. Was it a formal job with contract, or informal/by-project?
9. Who did you report to directly — a supervisor, owner, engineer?

**Memory triggers:**
- EncoreTools — tools? molds? fixtures? what material?
- MoldTech — injection molds? thermoforming? what machines?
- TDI (Tecnología & Diseño Industrial) — design-heavy? prototyping? production?

---

## BLOCK 3 — YOUR TITLE & REAL ROLE

> *Job titles don't always match what you actually did.*

10. What was your official title at each place?
11. What did you actually spend most of your day doing?
12. Were you more: operating machines / maintaining machines / programming machines / designing parts?
13. Did your role change over time — did you start doing one thing and end up doing more?

---

## BLOCK 4 — MACHINES & EQUIPMENT

> *Be specific — brand names, model numbers if you remember. These are gold on a CV.*

14. What CNC machines did you run or maintain? (lathe, mill, router, laser, plasma, EDM?)
15. Do you remember any brand or model? (Haas, Fanuc, GRBL, Mazak, DMG, other?)
16. What other machines were on the floor? (injection mold press, thermoforming, welding, grinder?)
17. Did you write or edit G-code? Or load programs others wrote?
18. Did you do setup — tool changes, offsets, zeroing, fixturing?

**Memory triggers:**
- Thermoforming plastics — was that EncoreTools or MoldTech?
- CNC milling on aluminum or steel — which shop?
- GRBL or Fanuc controller — do you remember which?

---

## BLOCK 5 — DAILY WORKFLOW

> *Walk me through a normal workday. Start to finish.*

19. When you arrived, what was the first thing you checked or did?
20. Who gave you the work orders — a supervisor, a planner, a drawing on paper?
21. What did you do when a machine had a problem? Who decided if it needed repair vs. adjustment?
22. Did you do any inspection or quality checks on parts?
23. Did you interact with design/engineering — or was that a different team?

---

## BLOCK 6 — TECHNICAL SPECIFICS

> *The details that make you stand out. Dig here.*

24. What materials did you work with most? (Nylamid, ABS, aluminum 6061, steel, other?)
25. Did you use SolidWorks or AutoCAD to reference drawings — or generate your own?
26. Did you do any CAM — setting toolpaths, feeds, speeds?
27. Did you weld? What process — MIG, TIG, spot, oxy-acetylene?
28. Did you do any mold repair, mold polishing, or mold assembly?
29. Did you work with hydraulics, pneumatics, or electrical panels on machines?

---

## BLOCK 7 — ACHIEVEMENTS & MOMENTS THAT STAND OUT

> *Think about a time something went better because you were there.*

30. Was there ever a machine breakdown you helped fix faster than expected?
31. Did you ever improve a process — even informally — that saved time or reduced scrap?
32. Was there a part or job that was particularly difficult and you pulled it off?
33. Did you train anyone, or become the go-to person for something specific?
34. Any metric you remember — cycle time, scrap rate, downtime, number of parts per shift?

---

## BLOCK 8 — COLLABORATION & STRUCTURE

35. Did you work alone or as part of a team on the floor?
36. Was there an engineer or designer you coordinated with regularly?
37. Did you ever deal with clients directly — deliveries, samples, approvals?
38. Were there formal procedures (traveler sheets, inspection logs, work orders) or was it informal?

---

## BLOCK 9 — ARTIFACTS & EVIDENCE

> *Things you made, modified, or have copies of.*

39. Do you have any parts, drawings, or photos from these jobs?
40. Did you write any procedures, maintenance logs, or setup sheets?
41. Do you have SolidWorks files or G-code files from work done there?
42. Are there any LinkedIn connections, references, or emails from those companies?

---

## BLOCK 10 — EXPANSION SLOTS

> *Things you're not sure about — mark them, don't skip them.*

43. Is there anything you did that you're not sure how to describe technically?
44. Was there a project or machine type you only touched briefly but want to mention?
45. Anything you learned at these jobs that you now use regularly?
46. What's the one thing from this experience that you're most proud of?
