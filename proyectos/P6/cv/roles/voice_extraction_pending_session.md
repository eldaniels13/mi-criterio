## TARGET 1 — MOLDTECH (priority)

### Block 2 — Company Context
- Team size on the floor (stint 1 vs stint 2)?
- Formal contract, payroll, or informal seasonal pay?
- Direct supervisor — name and role?
- Was MoldTech ISO-certified or operating under client (Continental) quality requirements?
- Any other clients beyond Continental and the medical fluid container line?

### Block 3 — Role
- Official title on contract (if any)?
- Day-to-day breakdown — % time on CNC vs press vs thermoforming vs metrology vs other?
- Did the role evolve between 2022 and 2024? (e.g., did you graduate from auxiliary tooling to programming or supervising newcomers?)

### Block 4 — Machines (concrete details)
- CNC brand and model? (Haas, Fanuc, generic? Mill or lathe? How many?)
- Thermoforming machine brand?
- Hydraulic press — manual foot-pedal or programmable?
- Did you write or edit G-code, or only load existing programs?

### Block 5 — Workflow
- Who issued work orders — planner, supervisor, drawings only?
- Quality inspection — was it your responsibility, dedicated QC, or shared?
- Interaction with the design/engineering team?
- How were drawings/specs delivered? (paper, PDF, drawings on the floor?)

### Block 6 — Technical specifics
- Plastic resin grades (Continental electrical isolation grade — name? medical container resin?)
- CAD software used (AutoCAD, SolidWorks, other)?
- CAM software — or only operator-level?
- Tolerance ranges achieved on molds?
- Welding done — process (MIG/TIG/stick), what kind of fixtures or assemblies?

### Block 7 — Achievements (the hard part)
- One mold or part you're particularly proud of finishing?
- A breakdown or rush job you helped resolve?
- Cycle time / scrap rate / parts-per-shift numbers (even rough)?
- Any process improvement you suggested or implemented?
- A tight tolerance or difficult geometry you nailed?

### Block 8 — Collaboration
- Solo vs team work pattern?
- Any direct contact with Continental staff or the medical client?
- Formal procedures (SOPs, inspection logs) or informal?

### Block 9 — Artifacts & references
- Any photos, drawings, samples kept?
- LinkedIn connections from MoldTech?
- Recommendation letter — possible/likely?
- Why didn't you stay longer / return permanently?

### Block 10 — Distinguishing 2022 vs 2024
- Most important difference between the two stints?
- Did you negotiate different pay or scope in 2024?
- Why half-time variable in 2024 — university load?
- Anything you'd want to add about the gap year (2023 = TDI)?
