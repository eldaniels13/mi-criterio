## Responsibilities

1. Plan and execute energy efficiency projects to reduce annual plant energy consumption
2. Achieve established energy performance targets
3. Ensure reliability and availability of: HVAC, ventilation, steam generation & distribution, fire protection, natural gas installations

5. Ensure compliance with insurance requirements and audit standards
6. Act as backup for Utilities Leader (steam generation systems)
